THE SYSTEM BEHIND THE WORK

GiNOVA presents Campi Flegrei as a DECISION PIPELINE showcase: multi-agent development, with review and revision through GiNOVA GATES™.

FROM TARGET TO CREATIVE DECISIONS

The recordings show the creative direction in sound. A later, dated design exercise makes the target, wording alternatives and quality questions explicit.

  1. 01

    Define the audience response

    The 30 April revision exercise sets a demanding ambition: an epic Italian supporters’ march, with a refrain memorable even to a ten-year-old fan.

    A concrete brief: who should remember it, and where it should work.

  2. 02

    Turn the target into choices

    The same exercise explores Hemingway-like directness, familiar stadium rhyme, clean verbs and repeatable call-and-response. The target becomes criteria for choosing words.

    Short language. A strong hook. Phrases a crowd can repeat.

  3. 03

    Give each contribution a brief

    A prepared Claude handoff defines the songwriter and stadium-chant architect role, musical constraints and five proposed quality questions. The handoff shows how a broad ambition can become a focused assignment.

    Proposed quality lens

    Memorability · rhythm · natural Italian · filler · immediate refrain impact

    Role, constraints and review questions in one assignment.

  4. 04

    Review. Revise. GiNOVA GATES™.

    GiNOVA describes its method as a multi-agent workflow within a multigate protocol. Review and revision connect creative contributions to the defined target.

    The target remains the reference for creative judgment.

  5. 05

    Make the choices inspectable

    The two provider records share one lyric script. Their contrasting arrangements, the Italian wording and the editorial translations let you examine how a media project takes shape.

    Listen to the arrangements. Read the language. Compare the decisions.

THE OUTPUT / TWO ARRANGEMENTS

One text. Two musical directions.

The original direction calls for terrace energy and electronic percussion. The orchestral direction calls for brass, strings and a dynamic build. Listen to the same words in each setting.

What the wording carries

Three details in the Italian text show how voice, memory and imagery work together.

01

“Noi” and the repeated image of fire place the refrain in a collective voice. The speaker belongs to a shared “we”.

02

Grandfather → father → speaker: the same act of crying passes through three generations. The repetition connects personal memory with inherited belonging.

03

The jersey becomes armour hidden beneath a shirt. An everyday garment carries an identity that remains present even when unseen.
