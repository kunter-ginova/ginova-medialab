    Agreed before production

    Scope, fee, revision rounds, deadline and usage rights are set in a written proposal. Filming crews, locations, talent, stock licences, media buying and placement are included only if explicitly agreed. Source files and exclusive rights are not transferred automatically.

    Working together

    From the first reference
    to the final frame.

    01

    Listen & define

    +

    Share the audience, message, channels, reference material, desired length and timing. We confirm the production approach and quote a defined scope, with named deliverables and an approval owner.

    02

    +

    Start with a musical sketch, visual concept or storyboard. Approve the direction before full production. New directions and additional outputs are quoted separately.

    03

    Shape & align

    +

    Review at the agreed milestones. The proposal sets the number of revision rounds and who consolidates feedback. A change of brief may change the price or schedule.

    04

    Prepare & deliver

    +

    Receive the agreed masters and versions. File formats, usage channels, territory, term, credits and source-file handover are confirmed in writing. Third-party and generated material are assessed for the intended use.
